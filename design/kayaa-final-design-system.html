01

Colour

Wisteria's palette, approved as-is except the accent. Peach and the honey-gold that replaced it are both retired at the client's request, in favour of a shade of blue. Lavender already sits next to blue on the colour wheel, so this is an analogous pairing rather than gold's complementary contrast — calmer and more cohesive, at the cost of natural pop. To keep it reading as a distinct accent rather than "more lavender," Periwinkle Sky is pulled cooler and bluer than the primary's purple-pink lean, so the two stay visually separate up close.

10

Accessibility & usage notes

Do

  • Keep body text at Ink or Ink Soft — never pure black, never below 14px
  • Use Primary Deep for every primary CTA, link and focus ring — one colour, one meaning
  • Reserve Periwinkle Sky for a single highlight per view
  • Keep tap targets at 44×44px minimum on mobile

Don't

  • Don't put body copy directly on Primary (#C3AFDA) — large/bold text only
  • Don't introduce a second accent colour without checking it against Periwinkle Sky first
  • Don't use the lift interaction on secondary/ghost buttons — it's the primary action's signature
  • Don't reintroduce green — including muted sage/olive tones in future icon or illustration work
