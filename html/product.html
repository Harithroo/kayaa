How to measure your baby

Measure height. With your baby lying flat, measure from the top of the head to the heel.

Check weight. The weight from the last clinic visit is fine.

Between two sizes? Go up. A slightly roomy fit is more comfortable in the heat.

    Questions parents ask

    How do I choose the right size?

    Our sizes follow age bands, but height and weight are the better guide. Check your baby against our size guide. If your baby is between two sizes, go up.

    How should I wash it?

    Please follow the care label. As a general guide, machine wash cold on a gentle cycle, tumble dry low or line dry in the shade, and do not bleach. To confirm confirm care instructions per product

    How long does delivery take?

    Colombo and suburbs usually take 1–2 working days and the rest of the island 2–4. Orders placed before 2pm on a working day are dispatched the same day To confirm confirm. See Delivery.

    What if it does not fit?

    Exchange any unworn, unwashed item with its tags on within 14 days of delivery. We cover the return courier cost on size swaps. See Returns & exchanges.

    How can I pay?

    At checkout you will see the payment options available. Card payments (Visa and Mastercard) are taken securely through our payment partner, so we never see your full card number.
