## Payment

You pay online by card (Visa and Mastercard) when you place your order. We do not offer cash on delivery, so you do not pay the courier anything.

## How delivery works

1. **Order placed.** You pay online and receive your order number.
2. **Packed.** We confirm and pack your order.
3. **On its way.** Your parcel goes with our courier. We will also WhatsApp you when it leaves us [[TODO: confirm]].
4. **Delivered.** Please check the parcel when it arrives.

## Tracking your order

Open [Track order](track.html) and enter your order number and the mobile number you used at checkout. You will see one of these statuses:

- **Pending:** we have your order and are checking it.
- **Confirmed:** your order is accepted and being prepared.
- **Shipped:** your parcel is on its way.
- **Delivered:** your parcel has arrived.
- **Cancelled:** the order was cancelled.

Payment has its own status: pending, paid, failed or refunded.

## Tips for a smooth delivery

- Add a landmark or a short direction in the delivery notes.
- Give a mobile number that someone can answer.
- Choose your district carefully: it sets your delivery estimate.
