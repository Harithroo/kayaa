## Information we collect

**When you place an order.** Your name, mobile number, delivery address, district and any delivery notes, what you ordered, and your email address if you choose to give one. Your mobile number is how the courier reaches you and how you can look up your order.

**When you create an account.** Your name, email address, mobile number and a password. We store your password in a scrambled (hashed) form, so nobody at Kayaa can read it. We also record whether you have confirmed your email address.

**When you write a review.** Your name, your rating and comments, and whether you bought the product from us, so we can show a "Verified purchase" label.

**When you contact us.** Your name, email address and message. If you message us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp's own privacy policy also applies to that conversation.

**When you pay.** You enter your card details on the secure payment page of our payment provider, Onepay. We do not see or store your full card number. We receive the payment status and a reference so we know whether your order is paid.

**Automatically.** Like most websites we receive technical information such as your IP address, browser, device and the pages you visit, and we use the cookies described below.

We do not ask for your baby's name, date of birth or photo. The clothing size you pick is used only to process your order.

## Keeping your information safe

Our website uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS), passwords are stored hashed, access to personal information is limited to people who need it, and repeated sign-in or form attempts are limited. No system is completely secure. If a personal data breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

## Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022 you can ask us to:

- give you a copy of the personal information we hold about you
- correct information that is wrong
- erase information we no longer need, subject to records we are required to keep
- restrict or stop some uses of your information
- stop using your information for direct marketing
- accept your withdrawal of consent where we rely on consent
