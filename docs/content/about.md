## What we care about

**Sized honestly.** Babies are not all the same size at the same age, so we recommend sizes by weight and height. Our [size guide](size-guide.html) shows you how to measure and what to choose.

**Soft on little skin.** Everyday pieces for newborns up to two years, made to be comfortable for long days and nights. [[TODO: fabric details for each range, only what is accurate]]

**Simple to shop.** Clear prices, secure card payment, delivery across Sri Lanka and an order you can track with your reference and mobile number. If something is not right, [returns are straightforward](returns.html).
