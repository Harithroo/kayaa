## What we care about

**Made for our weather.** Breathable cotton and bamboo pieces that stay comfortable in the heat. [[TODO: confirm the fabric claims, including any OEKO-TEX certification, before this goes live]]

**Sized by age, guided by height and weight.** Our sizes follow age bands, and our [size guide](size-guide.html) helps you check them against your baby's height and weight, so you can order once and it fits.

**Simple to shop.** Clear prices, secure card payment, island-wide delivery and an order you can track with your order number and mobile number. If the size is wrong, [swapping it is free](returns.html).
